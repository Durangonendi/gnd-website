Frequently asked questions

Where can I find used heavy equipment for sale in Turkey?

GND Machine Market publishes listings for used and new excavators, loaders, backhoe loaders, dozers, graders and rollers. You can browse the live listings on this page or send us the machine you are looking for.

Can GND source new or used machines that are not listed?

Yes. GND Machinery sources new and used equipment. Send brand, model, year range and budget on WhatsApp and we will prepare an offer.

What should I check before buying a used excavator or loader?

Check engine hours and model year, hydraulic leaks, undercarriage or tire wear, pin and bushing play, service records and the serial number plate. See our used excavator buying checklist.

Is listing a machine free?

Yes, creating a listing is free. If a commission applies to a sale it is determined according to the product sold and notified to the parties before the transaction.

Do you handle export?

Contact us on WhatsApp with the machine, destination country and timeline. Shipping and export terms are agreed per transaction.
