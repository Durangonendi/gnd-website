Sell Your Heavy Equipment: Free Listing

How to list your excavator, loader, dozer or spare parts and reach buyers.

Sell your heavy equipment

Listing is free. Excavators, loaders, backhoe loaders, dozers, graders, rollers, telehandlers, attachments and spare parts can be listed on GND Machine Market and appear in the machine categories and on the home page.

List Your Machine for Free →

Step by step

  1. Sign up or log in. You can edit your listings later.
  2. Fill in the details: brand, model, year, hours, condition and price.
  3. Add photos: all four sides, cab interior, engine bay and the hour meter.
  4. Wait for review. GND reviews the listing and publishes it if suitable.
  5. Talk to buyers. Communication is directly between you and the buyer.

Tips for faster sales

  • Show a clear price instead of "call".
  • Enter engine hours and model year accurately.
  • Use bright, sharp photos and mention service records.
  • Disclose known faults; it speeds up negotiation and avoids disputes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sell my heavy equipment?

Create a free listing on GND Machine Market with brand, model, year, hours, condition, price and clear photos. GND reviews the listing before it goes live.

Is listing free? Is there a commission?

Creating a listing is free. If a commission applies to a sale it is determined according to the product sold and notified to the parties in writing before the transaction. No fee is applied unless you accept it.

What details make a listing sell faster?

State a clear price, exact engine hours and model year, include sharp photos from all sides, the cab, the engine bay and the hour meter, and disclose known faults.

Can I edit my listing later?

Yes, from My Account. An edited listing is reviewed again before it is published.

Can I send details on WhatsApp instead of filling in the form?

Yes. Send photos and details to +90 555 070 80 34.
