How to identify the right part

  1. Note the machine brand, model and serial number from the machine plate.
  2. Photograph the old part and any part number stamped on it.
  3. Ask for OEM, aftermarket and used options with price and warranty differences.
  4. For expensive parts, ask the seller for photos showing the actual condition.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy heavy equipment spare parts in Turkey?

GND Machine Market lists new, used and salvaged spare parts. If you cannot find a part, send the machine brand, model, serial number and part number on WhatsApp.

What information do I need to order a spare part?

Machine brand, model, year and serial number, plus the part number if available. A photo of the old part speeds up identification.

What is the difference between OEM, aftermarket and used parts?

OEM parts come from the machine manufacturer or its authorized supplier. Aftermarket parts are made by another manufacturer to do the same job. Used or salvaged parts are removed from another machine. Price, service life and warranty differ for each.

Can I sell spare parts on GND Machine Market?

Yes. Create a listing, choose the spare parts category and add brand, model, part number, condition and photos. Listings are reviewed by GND before publishing and listing is free.
