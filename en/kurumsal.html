From the Founder

Duran Gönendi

Founder and Chairman of the Board

Duran Gönendi began his entrepreneurial journey in the heavy equipment industry in 2019 with a sole proprietorship. Building on the experience and commercial track record he gained in the sector, he founded GND İş Makineleri Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. in 2024.

Today, Duran Gönendi leads the company's management and growth processes, working to establish GND as a strong brand in heavy equipment and international sourcing through a service approach grounded in reliability, quality, and customer satisfaction.
