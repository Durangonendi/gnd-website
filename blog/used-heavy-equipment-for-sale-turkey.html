Used Heavy Equipment for Sale in Turkey

Buyers searching for used heavy equipment for sale in Turkey usually expect a dealer lot with fixed stock. That is not how the Turkish market actually works, and it is not how GND operates either. Instead, real availability changes by the day as machines are listed, sold, and sourced on demand. The most accurate way to see what is genuinely for sale right now is GND's own Machine Market — a live listing board rather than a static catalog.

What "for sale near me" actually means in cross-border sourcing

Heavy equipment trade rarely works like a local car lot. A machine physically sitting in Turkey may be the right fit for a buyer in Kenya, Spain, or Bangladesh once transport and destination compliance are worked out — proximity to the machine matters far less than whether the machine matches the job and the paperwork is in order. So instead of a map pin, the useful question is: what is actually listed and available today? That is exactly what a live marketplace answers and a static product page cannot.

Browse live listings on the GND Machine Market

The GND Machine Market (Pazar) shows real listings posted by sellers — machines, attachments, and spare parts — with the asking price, condition notes, and photos the seller provided, updated in real time. If nothing currently listed matches what you need, you can post a buy request instead and get matched with sellers or GND's own sourcing network as new stock appears.

Before buying: verify the machine, not just the price

A low asking price means little without knowing service history, real working hours, undercarriage or tire wear, and whether the attachment package matches what is shown. GND has a separate, detailed checklist for this — see How to Import Used Construction Equipment from Turkey for the full buyer's due-diligence process before you commit to any listing.

Why there is no fixed price list

Prices for the same model can differ significantly based on model year, working hours, maintenance records, attachments included, undercarriage or tire condition, and current exchange rates. Publishing a generic price table would be misleading rather than helpful. The reliable approach is to look at what sellers are actually asking for real, current machines on the Machine Market, or to post the exact specification you need as a buy request and receive offers against it.
